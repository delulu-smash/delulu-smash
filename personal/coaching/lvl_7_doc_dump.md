THE LITTLE MAC GUIDE



Normals -



Jab: frame 1, -12 on shield, used for jab locks, catching jumps off of ledge and rapid jab/gentleman jab



Gentleman jab: frame traps into up smash/side b, -19 on shield. Can set up onto platforms around 40-50 if di’d in.



Rapid jab: used to get consistent 20 damage, helps you avoid punishes after using a move that isn’t safe on shield. -25 on shield



F tilt: first hit is frame 4, second hit is frame 12, used for shield pressure, burst option, tech situations, ledge trapping. Kills very early, -17 on shield



Up tilt: frame 4, both back and front hit set up onto platforms around 50, combos into up air, grounded up b, aerial up b at mid percents and another up tilt at low percents. Used to pressure shields safely if positioned correctly, -18 on shield



Down tilt: frame 3, sets up onto platforms around 70-80, combos into grounded and aerial up b around 70-100. Starting at 40 it combos into side b while ending at around 70,  down tilt will start comboing into up side b which allows you to continue to hit the combo at around 70-80. Used for shield pressure, 2 framing, ledge trapping, and tech situations. -14 on shield



Dash attack: frame 7, burst option that sets up onto platforms around 20. Can cross up but is not consistent. Can 2 frame and hit below ledge. Frame traps into itself while also frame trapping into up smash. -16 on shield.



F smash -



Straight: frame 14, earliest killing, used for hard reads or armoring through opponents attack for a heavy punish. Rarely leads into a tech situation but normally does around 5-7. -16 on shield.



Uppercut: frame 14, sends upwards and is somewhat disjointed. Used to challenge and contest strong aerials and landing options. Sets up onto platforms around 10-15. -16 on shield.



Bodyblow: frame 15, sends low to the ground, used to put opponent in tech situations to rack up more damage. Does the most damage of the 3 and allows for 50/50’s and frame traps depending on where the opponent lands. -13 on shield. Breaks shields very well.





Up smash: frame 10, has a small sweetspot that does more damage and has higher knockback. Used as an anti-air and an oos option that comes out on frame 9. Hits above sbf and bf platforms but barely hits above ps2 platforms. -23 on shield. 



Down smash: first hit is frame 10 and second hit is frame 17, used as a 2 framing option. Lowest knockback of all smash attacks but hits below ledge and sets up into tech situations from around 20-35, covers almost every option during a tech situation and sends at a nasty angle when the 2 frame is hit. The first hit is faster for the 2 frame but the second hit has less end lag and allows you to react to the opponents options faster. First hit is -23 on shield and the second hit is -16 on shield.



Aerials - 



Nair: frame 2, negative on hit, sourspot on his back has actual knockback. Used for combo breaking and aerial boost recovery. -8 on shield, allows you to cross up very easily but has almost no shield damage. 



Fair: frame 10, sets into tech situations around 50-80, can be used for aerial boost recovery but has more endlag than fair/dair. If a fair is hit it can true combo into a down smash or a possible jab lock into ko punch/any smash attack. -10 on shield



Bair: frame 11, sets into tech situations around 40-70, can be used for aerial boost recovery but is extremely slow and laggy. Has the same properties as fair to where it hits very low and can allow you to tech chase/jab lock into a heavy punish. -13 on shield



Dair: frame 7, allows you to reset/jab lock on platforms, helps with aerial boost recovery. Mostly used to reset on platforms but also allows you to buffer a frame 1 turn around when you land onto the platform. This helps you land more niche sets ups such as reverse up tilt into ko punch without having the risk of messing up the reverse up tilt. -15 on shield



Specials - 



Straight lunge (neutral b): initial power charge is frame 4, charge ends at frame 123. Used in disadvantage, neutral, and shield break punishes. Straight lunge allows you to armor through multi hits such as palu nair, not just multi hits but also low damaging/staled moves such as steve up tilt or duck hunt can. Straight lunge can also armor through projectiles such as link bomb, bayonetta gun, and dr Mario pill. Straight lunge is also shield cancelable which means it can be canceled into shield as well as up smash/up b. Since it is shield cancelable you can also jump and do aerials, side b, and even another straight lunge to reset the power charge. - on shield depends on how much it is charged.



Jolt Haymaker (side b): frame 8 (fast version) and the regular version is frame 25. The move can be thrown out faster when you press b right after the side b starts. move is mostly used as a true combo out of d tilt, jolt haymaker has set ups so that you can snap to ledge and 2 frame while also catching jumps. This allows for a ledge trump side b which can kill at 70 while also still being able to recover. This move is used to kill very early at ledge after 2 framing/hitting the opponent with a d tilt. -36 on shield (fast version) -39 on shield (regular version). Note that aerial side b is -31 on shield (fast version) and -32 on shield (regular version)



Rising uppercut (up b): frame 3, Mac’s best oos option. Frame 1 invincibility which allows you to interrupt moves such as rob up air and Ganon nair. Can be used as a combo breaker and also kills very early. Both aerial version and grounded can kill very early if set up on platforms correctly. Both d tilt and both sides of up b can combo into up b. D tilt combos into both dash up b and jump up b (jump up b kills earlier). Both hits of up tilt confirm into up b but it is better to use grounded up b for the front hit and aerial up b for the back hit. 



Slip counter (down b): frame 5, decent knockback and range. Lingers for a surprisingly long time. You can slide forward while using counter if you move the stick back the way you were dashing allowing for more range. Allows mac to stall offstage while also countering any projectile/item thrown at him during the counters linger. Counter will not stop you from moving towards the blastzone. The counter hit starts on frame 16.



Grabs -



Standing grab: frame 9, active for 2 frames. Very small range, very situation and should only be used during ledge trapping or as an oos option at 30-50.



Dash grab: frame 13, active for 1 frame. Horrible range and just sucks in every aspect. This should never be used unless you are trying to dash grab in the direction that you aren’t facing. 



Pivot grab: frame 14, active for 1 frame. Has more range than the regular grabs but is also pretty situational. Should only be used to read rolls.



Pummel: frame 1, racks up damage relatively fast. 



Throws - 



Forward throw: sets up into tech situations around 30-40 if undi’d and ledge trapping/stage control. Kills 180+



Back throw: sets up onto platforms around 40 but extremely situation and inconsistent. Used for ledge trapping/stage control. Kills at 150+



Up throw: used to set up juggle situations (LMAO), Mac’s worst throw and has almost no use. Kills 230+



Down throw: Mac’s best throw, sets up onto platforms around 50-70 depending on weight. Has a 50/50 around 80 where if they di in they get hit by up b and if they di they can die to jolt haymaker. This is very good at ledge because it can get you kills very early but also guarantees damage. Mac’s down throw still combos into up b around 110 but only if they di in.



Tech - 



Boost grab (roll cancel grab): increases Mac’s grab range to Samus’s grab range. Inputs are shield grab and initial dash all at the same time



Aerial boost recovery: increases Mac’s magnet hands by a small amount that allows you to recover from a further distance. Inputs are jump, any aerial, immediate up b



Instasnap up b: allows mac to snap to the ledge instantly with up b.
