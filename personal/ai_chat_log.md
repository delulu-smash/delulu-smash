In Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, every fighter has a 3-frame jumpsquat. They leave the ground and become airborne on frame 4.
pydantic-ai ➤ how fast can you jump out of shield
In Smash Ultimate, you can jump directly out of shield with a 3-frame jumpsquat, becoming airborne on frame 4.

For an aerial out of shield, its effective startup is generally:

3 frames of jumpsquat + the aerial’s startup

For example, a frame-3 aerial can hit on approximately frame 6 out of shield.
pydantic-ai ➤ do you get shield drop penalty for jumping?
No. In Smash Ultimate, jumping bypasses the normal 11-frame shield-drop animation.

Your 3-frame jumpsquat begins directly from shield, so you become airborne on frame 4. The shield-drop delay applies when you simply release shield before performing an option that
cannot be used directly out of shield.
pydantic-ai ➤ can hold buffe a jump out of shield
Yes. In Smash Ultimate, you can hold-buffer jump during shieldstun, and it will begin on the first actionable frame after shieldstun ends. You can continue holding shield—the jump
will cancel shield directly, avoiding the 11-frame shield-drop animation.

Using a dedicated jump button is generally more reliable than tap jump.
pydantic-ai ➤ what else can you hold buffer out of shield stun
After shieldstun in Smash Ultimate, you can buffer these options directly out of shield:

 • Jump
 • Up special
 • Up smash
 • Grab — subject to the shield-grab startup penalty
 • Spot dodge
 • Forward or backward roll
 • Aerial attack by buffering jump and attack; jump occurs first

Most other grounded actions—such as tilts, jabs, down smash, or dash—require you to undergo the 11-frame shield-drop animation first. Inputs can generally be held during shieldstun so
the legal option begins on the first actionable frame.

pydantic-ai ➤ what is frame data on little macs nair
Little Mac’s neutral aerial (Nair) in Smash Ultimate:

 • Startup: Frame 2
 • Active: Frame 2 only
 • Total duration: 15 frames
 • Landing lag: 10 frames
 • Damage: 2%
 • Out-of-shield: Can hit on approximately frame 5 after the 3-frame jumpsquat

It is extremely fast but has very short range and low reward. Source: Ultimate Frame Data and SmashWiki.
pydantic-ai ➤ what does landing lag mean above
Landing lag is the period after you touch the ground during an aerial attack when your character cannot act.

For Little Mac’s Nair:

 • If Mac lands while Nair is still in the state that triggers landing lag, he is unable to act for 10 frames.
 • After those frames, he can shield, attack, jump, move, etc.
 • If the aerial autocancels, the normal 10-frame aerial landing lag is avoided, and Mac uses the much shorter standard landing animation instead.

This matters for safety on shield: even though Nair starts on frame 2, landing after using it can leave Mac punishable.
pydantic-ai ➤ "shorter standard landing animation" how many frames is that
In Smash Ultimate, an autocanceled aerial uses normal landing lag instead:

 • Soft landing: 2 frames
 • Hard landing: 4 frames, usually when landing with greater downward speed

So Little Mac avoids Nair’s 10-frame landing lag and instead experiences typically 2–4 frames, depending on the landing. My earlier wording was simplified.
pydantic-ai ➤ so auto cancel still get landing lag
Yes. Autocanceling avoids the aerial’s specific landing lag, but you still experience the normal landing animation:

 • Soft landing: 2 frames
 • Hard landing: 4 frames

So “autocancel” does not mean zero lag—it means normal landing lag instead of, for example, Little Mac Nair’s 10-frame aerial landing lag.
pydantic-ai ➤ what conditions does macs nair auto cancel
Little Mac’s Nair autocancels if he lands:

 • On frame 1 — before the hitbox appears
 • On frame 16 or later — after the move’s 15-frame animation has finished

If he lands during frames 2–15, he receives Nair’s 10 frames of aerial landing lag. In normal play, the useful autocancel is usually letting the move finish before touching the
ground.
pydantic-ai ➤ how long in air if you full sprint, short hop fast fall nair
On flat ground, a running short-hop → earliest fast-fall Nair with Little Mac keeps him airborne for about 22 frames.

Including his 3-frame jumpsquat, he lands roughly 25 frames after the jump input.

 • Running speed does not change vertical airtime.
 • Nair itself does not alter his trajectory.
 • If you fast-fall later than the first opportunity, the sequence takes longer.
 • Since Nair finishes after 15 frames, this timing normally allows it to autocancel before landing.
