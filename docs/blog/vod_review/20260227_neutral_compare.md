## Pre-Watch Analysis

Below details my understand of [Yoshi](#yoshi) and how may approach neutral

Highlights of [Yoshi](#yoshi):
1. spaced tilts on shield if keep low profile can some what be safe, but generally will nair or ariel out of shield which can hit many times
1. If Yoshi tries to grab out of shield (eg you daf smash shield), can usually side B over and hit
1. Yoshi largely wants to approach from air, especially with a nair or down air (which can set up tech chases). We can neutral B the down air
1. Yoshi really want to get kills either via edge guard or up air juggle. Can be hard to land against Yoshi given cant neutral B up air, and it can kill decently early if get hit high
1. Stage usually like without high plats personally like not super long stages, as like to push aggression once get a hit in neutral. an FD like stage can be good
1. Do not jump when hit off stage unless you want to be dair spiked (that hitbox is deceiving).
1. Eggs are quite reactable with shield (so not much of threat)
1. Dash attack is quite reactable
1. Good to keep in mind Yoshi's special characteristics:
    1. Super Armour when jump
    1. Cant be shield poked
1. If Up tilt out early percents can usually nair quickly given uptilt is negative on hit at early percents. Many Yoshi's I face ariel attack out of disadvantage
1. If you don't tech in this MU you will die very early. Your ability to reset neutral is key as goes from manageable vs snowballing (racking up large damage quick off one interaction)
1. Recovery generally like recovering low and close to stage (helps avoid his nair and dair well)


What does this mean for neutral.
1. Yoshis threat/burst range given ability to react on shield or movement (eg dash away), is not great. So he wants to approach from plats and/or air many times.
1. Neutral I like to take slow (so can easily react to options chosen), and using uptilt and uaf smash as anti air.
1. lot of bait and punish (use plats at times to bait out plat drop ariel attack)
1. In neutral I may do some MU check items (eg do they think can grab OOS).
1. Abuse super armour on his ariels
